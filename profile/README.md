# drooly.ai

Personal intelligence for culture, creation, strategy, and independent digital identity.

We build focused products at the intersection of AI, music, social protocols, and
creator-owned infrastructure. The work is practical, security-conscious, and
explicit about what is live versus experimental.

## Products and research

- **[DROOLY.AI](https://drooly.ai)** — culture and music intelligence.
- **[ICEFAM.FM](https://icefam.fm)** — the pre-launch social music and web3 radio platform.
- **[Icefam Records](https://icefamrecords.com)** — independent music and artist infrastructure.
- **[WokeSocial](https://woke.social)** — portable social-state protocol research.
- **[The Continuity Bureau](https://www.continuitybureau.com)** — decentralization doctrine and continuity research.

## Operating principles

- Ship useful software and label beta systems honestly.
- Keep private credentials, wallet secrets, and unpublished media out of repositories.
- Prefer user-controlled identity, portable state, and replaceable infrastructure.
- Treat safety, provenance, moderation, and access control as product requirements.
- Never manufacture engagement, token activity, or investment expectations.

Security reports belong in the private process described by our
[security policy](../SECURITY.md), not in public issues.
